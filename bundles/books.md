VILE BOOKS AS DANGEROUS AS NARCOTICS, POPE SAYS VATICAN CITY, June 11 (AP) Pope Pius XII today sharply attacked books that emphasize vice. He told delegates to a recent world congress of editors at Florence the danger from such books was not restricted to children. Severe penalties Imposed by law against such books are as Justified as are those against the traffic of narcotics, the Pope continued. Such books, he said, produce dangerous excitation that "sometimes totally paralyzes higher faculties and produces a permanent disorder, an artificial need of passionate character that at times reaches a real aberration."
